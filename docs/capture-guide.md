# Room Photograph Capture Guide

## Best Practices for AI Furniture Detection & Reconstruction

1. **Camera Height & Angle:**
   - Hold the phone at eye level (~1.5m) tilted slightly downward ($15^\circ–25^\circ$) to capture both the furniture footprint and the floor-wall boundary.
   - Avoid extreme low-angle or bird's-eye shots.

2. **Lighting Conditions:**
   - Turn on ceiling lights and open blinds to reduce harsh shadows under sofas and tables.
   - Avoid direct backlighting facing into windows (which causes silhouette saturation).

3. **Coverage & Framing:**
   - Capture at least two adjacent corners of the room in the frame.
   - Ensure furniture feet or floor contacts are visible without being cut off by the camera boundary.

4. **Multi-View Capture:**
   - Take 2–4 overlapping views of the room from different vantage points.
   - The app's identity review grouping will consolidate multiple observations of the same physical item.
